Your 2D + 3D Game Lab

Six hands-on projects accompany chapters 11-18. Keep the whole games folder together.

Before you play

Serve this folder over HTTP. From the textbook folder, run node scripts/serve.mjs and open http://127.0.0.1:8000/games/. The 2D projects work without internet once served locally. The 3D projects download pinned Three.js and Rapier libraries unless you use the optional vendor script.

Ch. 11-12Collision PlaygroundMove shapes and compare contact with overlap.Ch. 12-14Robot OrchardCollect every gem, avoid bugs, and reach the portal.Ch. 15-16Robot Island ExplorerExplore a 3D island and collect four crystals.Ch. 17Rapier Drop LabCompare bounce, gravity, and the shape of a collider.Ch. 18Marble QuestRoll, push crates, collect five gems, and enter the gate.Ch. 163D Asset WorkshopChange a robot's colors and export your own GLB.

These are learning-sized desktop/keyboard projects with on-screen movement buttons, not production engines. The textbook explains their collision limits and extension ideas.
